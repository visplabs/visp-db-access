This server gives guarded access to production databases. Call list_databases first. Use search_schema and describe_table to learn the structure. Call check_sql before run_query when you are not sure a statement is accepted. Every call needs a `task`: one sentence that says what you are doing and why. You can read data; you cannot change it on your own. To change data, call propose_write: people check, approve and run the write, and you follow it with get_approval. When a call is refused, read `error.fix` and do what it says. Do not retry a refused statement unchanged.
