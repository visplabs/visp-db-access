# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any project space: issues,
  pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks these rules, contact the maintainers privately
through the [visplabs organization](https://github.com/visplabs) on GitHub.
Do not open a public issue for it. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break
these rules, and may block a person who repeats the behaviour.
